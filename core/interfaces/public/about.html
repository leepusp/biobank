CEPID B3 Research Network

About B3 LIMS

B3 LIMS is the laboratory information management and public catalog environment supporting the organization, preservation, traceability and controlled distribution of biological resources maintained within CEPID B3.

CEPID B3

The Center for Research, Innovation and Dissemination in Bacterial and Bacteriophage Biology (CEPID B3) is a collaborative research network involving researchers from USP, UNESP, UNICAMP and UNIFESP across the state of São Paulo.

The network investigates molecular mechanisms affecting bacterial cell biology, multicellular behavior, competition, colonization, antibiotic resistance and interactions among bacteria, eukaryotic hosts and bacteriophages.

Biobank Mission

CEPID B3 research activities generate biological resources of substantial scientific and technological value, including bacteria of environmental and clinical origin, bacteriophages and molecular vectors.

B3 LIMS supports standardized, secure and traceable stewardship of these resources, contributing to biosafety, legal compliance and long-term sustainability of the scientific collection.

Scientific and Institutional Relevance

The CEPID B3 Biobank contributes to the preservation, digital organization and dissemination of scientific collections that are strategic for research and technological development.

Consolidating the repository strengthens research reproducibility, experimental data integrity and the generation of new innovation opportunities, including antimicrobial compounds and therapeutic targets.

Outreach and Social Impact

The initiative also supports scientific communication, documentation and inclusive dissemination activities designed to broaden access to knowledge in microbiology and biological sciences.

Institutional Benefits

B3 LIMS provides an open and maintainable digital foundation for biological resource management and may support adoption and collaboration across additional research groups and institutions.
