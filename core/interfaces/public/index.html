Explore and understand the catalog

Search the public catalog by Collection name, organism, Sample type or public Tag. Catalog visibility is independent from internal inventory access.

Public statistics and visualizations include only records that have passed the canonical public disclosure boundary. Private, embargoed, inactive and trashed Samples are excluded before aggregation.

Public metadata does not represent authorization to receive biological material. Material access and Shipment intake remain governed workflows.
