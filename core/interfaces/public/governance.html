Governance, Biosafety and Quality

B3 LIMS supports the technical and administrative governance framework of the CEPID B3 Biobank, including biosafety, traceability, collection quality and institutional compliance.

Infrastructure and Cryopreservation

Biological Samples are preserved using standardized cryogenic tubes, boxes and racks in cryopreservation systems operating at −80 °C.

The repository uses redundancy strategies and independent storage equipment to reduce operational risks. Supporting infrastructure includes environmental control and dedicated emergency power resources.

Biosafety and Standard Operating Procedures

Receiving, handling, storage, processing, monitoring and disposal activities are governed by documented Standard Operating Procedures.

Applicable biosafety requirements, equipment validation and personnel training are incorporated into the repository governance process.

Authentication, Quality Control and Traceability

Biological material may undergo screening, authentication, quality control and associated metadata review before inclusion in managed collections.

Traceability is supported through unique identifiers, barcodes and QR-based workflows maintained through B3 LIMS.

Access and Use Policy

Public metadata access does not represent unrestricted access to biological material.

Material access remains subject to institutional authorization, applicable biosafety requirements, governance review and the required material transfer procedures.

Legal Compliance and Institutional Integration

Repository activities are designed to operate in accordance with applicable Brazilian requirements for genetic resources, including SisGen-related obligations when applicable.

B3 LIMS also provides a digital foundation for future integration with institutional and scientific collection management initiatives.
